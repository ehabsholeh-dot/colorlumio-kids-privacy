Privacy Policy for ColorLumio Kidz

Last updated: September 30, 2026

ColorLumio Kidz ("the App") is a children's coloring application designed to provide a simple, creative, and enjoyable coloring experience.

We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how ColorLumio Kidz handles information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

ColorLumio Kidz does not require users to create an account, provide a name, email address, phone number, or other personal information in order to use the App.

The App does not intentionally collect or transmit personal information from users.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Some app settings and coloring progress may be stored locally on your device so that the App can provide its features correctly.

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to us.

3. Children's Privacy

ColorLumio Kidz is designed to be suitable for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

The App does not require children to provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

4. Internet Connection

The core coloring features of ColorLumio Kidz are designed to work without requiring users to submit personal information over the internet.

We do not operate a server that receives personal information from users through the App.

5. Third-Party Services

ColorLumio Kidz does not currently use third-party advertising, analytics, social login, or user account services to collect personal information.

If third-party services are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain how those services handle information.

6. Data Security

Because ColorLumio Kidz does not intentionally collect or transmit personal information, there is no personal user database operated by us through the App.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Since we do not collect personal information through the App, we do not maintain personal user accounts or personal information that requires deletion from our servers.

Locally stored app data can be removed by uninstalling the App or by clearing the App's data through the Android device settings, where applicable.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if the App's features or data practices change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
